# H. pylori data recovery and rerun

Date: 2026-09-22

After the MoneyCat disk remount, `/Volumes/MoneyCat/Data/song_2026_hpylori/`
was missing. The cohort was rebuilt at that path and all recorded cagPAI and
metadata analyses were rerun from the restored FASTA and workbook inputs.

## Restored inputs

- 528/528 NCBI assemblies listed in `cagpai_states_extended.tsv` were
  redownloaded from NCBI into `genomes/` (868,243,951 bp of FASTA total).
- The cagA, vacA and CARD collaborator workbooks were restored from the local
  WeChat received-file cache.
- `metadata.csv` was reconstructed from the committed 528-row merged analysis
  table, preserving assembly, country, FastBAPS lineage, disease group,
  phylogenetic population and PC1-PC3.
- A manifest with file counts and workbook checksums is in
  `RECOVERY_MANIFEST.tsv` on MoneyCat.

## Reruns

1. Circular-origin-filtered structural calls:
   `syn2bani struct --bed --circular NC_000915.1` for 528/528 genomes.
2. Unfiltered structural calls for the threshold sensitivity analysis:
   528/528 genomes.
3. 28-marker cagPAI calling with minimap2 2.31 (`-cx asm20`): 528/528 genomes.
4. Circular-origin-filtered extended-state classification.
5. cagA/vacA/CARD metadata association and lineage-stratified tests.
6. Artifact-threshold sensitivity and reverse-complement diagnostic.
7. Three-way locality classification of rearranged complete islands.
8. Forensic review of the 26 cagPAI/cagA-discordant genomes.
9. Regeneration of Figure 8 and Supplementary Figure S11.

## Validation against committed results

The rerun reproduced the recorded results exactly at the analytical-table
level:

- `cagpai_states.tsv`: 528 rows, identical to the committed table.
- `cagpai_states_extended_filtered.tsv`: 528 rows, identical to the committed
  filtered table.
- `merged_genome_table.tsv`: 528 genomes x 55 columns, identical to the
  committed table; merge losses were zero.
- Association, sensitivity, locality and discordant-review outputs were
  byte-equivalent or numerically identical to the committed files. The only
  text difference was label ordering (`AG/NAG` versus `NAG/AG`) in section
  titles of `cagpai_association_stratified.tsv`; all counts and statistics were
  unchanged.
